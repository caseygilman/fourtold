# FOURTOLD — Master Project Brief v1.0

## 1. Project Name

**FOURTOLD**

Working visual brand:

**FOUR†OLD**

The cross is not decorative. It is central to the meaning of the product.

---

## 2. Core Meaning

The name **Fourtold** carries several ideas at once:

- **Foretold** — what is to come has already been revealed.
- **Four** — the four central pillars of daily stewardship:
  - Faith
  - Fitness
  - Finance
  - Family
- **Cross-centered life** — represented by the cross within the wordmark.
- **Daily response** — because what has been foretold should shape how we live today.

The central philosophical question of the app is:

> **Knowing what has been foretold, how then shall I live today?**

Primary brand line:

> **Live today in light of what is to come.**

Alternative / supporting phrasing:

> **Live faithfully today in light of what is to come.**

---

## 3. Product Vision

Fourtold is a Christian formation app designed to help a person live deliberately and faithfully across the major areas of life.

It is not primarily a habit tracker.

It is not a productivity app with Christian language added on top.

It is not a system for earning favor with God.

It is a daily framework for translating conviction into action.

The intended experience is:

1. Remember what is true.
2. Remember who you are called to be.
3. Recognize the responsibilities in front of you.
4. Pick up your crosses.
5. Walk faithfully today.

---

## 4. Theological Foundation

This is a non-negotiable foundation of the product:

**Salvation is by God's grace, not by works.**

The app must never imply that:

- completing tasks earns salvation
- completing tasks earns God's love
- streaks indicate spiritual worth
- levels indicate holiness
- productivity equals righteousness
- failure to complete a day means failure before God

Works are understood as a response to grace and an opportunity to glorify God.

The product philosophy can be summarized as:

> **Grace first. Then walk.**

The launch experience reinforces this explicitly.

Current launch sequence:

1. **YOU ARE NOT SAVED BY YOUR WORKS.**
2. **YOU ARE SAVED BY GOD'S GRACE.**
3. **YOUR WORKS ARE MEANT SOLELY TO GLORIFY HIM.**
4. **FOUR†OLD**
5. **Live today in light of what is to come.**

The app should continually preserve the distinction between:

**spiritual worth**

and

**faithful daily practice**

Fourtold may measure the second.

It must never claim to measure the first.

## 6. Crosses

Actions inside Fourtold are not merely called tasks.

They are called:

**Crosses**

This is inspired by the call to take up one's cross and follow Christ.

A Cross is a concrete action a person chooses or accepts for the day in response to conviction, responsibility, or calling.

Examples:

- Pray before touching the phone.
- Read one chapter of Scripture.
- Complete today's workout.
- Send the difficult email.
- Spend 20 focused minutes building income.
- Have an intentional conversation with your spouse.
- Apologize without defending yourself.
- Put the phone away during dinner.
- Take your child somewhere one-on-one.

The intent is not to dramatize ordinary tasks.

The language exists to remind the user:

**faithfulness often requires willingly carrying something difficult.**

---

## 7. Today's Walk

The central daily experience is:

# Today's Walk

This is where the user sees the Crosses chosen for the current day.

The intended V0.1 loop is:

**Four Pillars → Crosses → Today's Walk → Complete → Persist locally**

The daily experience should feel simple.

The user should not need to navigate a complicated dashboard before taking action.

The app should repeatedly answer:

> **What does faithfulness look like today?**

---

## 8. Completion Philosophy

Completing a Cross should feel meaningful without becoming spiritually manipulative.

Completion means:

> **I did what I said I would do today.**

It does not mean:

> **God is more pleased with me now.**

It does not mean:

> **I am more righteous than someone else.**

It does not mean:

> **My spiritual level increased in God's eyes.**

The system should reward consistency, intentionality, courage, and faithfulness.

It should not reward comparison-based spiritual ego.

---

## 9. Gamification

Gamification is part of the Fourtold vision, but it must serve formation rather than addiction or vanity.

Possible mechanics include:

- experience
- levels
- progression
- streaks
- avatar development
- pillar growth
- milestones
- badges
- seasons
- disciplines
- challenges

The central rule:

> **Gamification represents practice, not spiritual worth.**

A high-level user is not portrayed as more loved by God.

A low-level user is not portrayed as spiritually inferior.

Progress represents accumulated acts of intentional practice.

Possible directions:

- appearance changes with progression
- armor or clothing develops
- tools or symbolic items unlock
- pillar-specific visual elements emerge
- environments change
- posture or presence changes
- cross-bearing symbolism develops

The avatar should communicate:

**formation over time**

rather than:

**fantasy power accumulation**

The visual system should remain grounded in the spiritual philosophy of the app.

## 11. Disciple-Level Concept

A humorous early concept involved progression levels named after biblical disciples.

Examples could include playful references such as:

- Thomas level
- Peter level
- John level
- Paul level

This is currently exploratory and intentionally lighthearted.

It may eventually become:

- an Easter egg
- an internal progression system
- a humorous badge system

The app must never imply that one biblical person was spiritually "worth more" than another in a simplistic ranking system.

The humor is useful.

The theology still matters.

## 24. AI Role

AI could eventually help the user convert intentions into concrete actions.

Example:

User:

> "I need to become a better husband."

Fourtold might help convert that into:

**Family Cross**

> Put your phone away and give your wife 30 uninterrupted minutes tonight.

Or:

User:

> "I'm stressed about money."

Fourtold might generate:

**Finance Cross**

> Spend 25 focused minutes applying to one higher-paying role.

AI should help create actionable clarity.

It should not pretend to speak for God.

It should not tell the user:

> "God wants you to do X."

A safer framing is:

> "Based on the priorities you've told Fourtold matter to you..."

---

## 25. Product Guardrails

Fourtold should never:

- claim divine authority
- claim to know God's specific will
- quantify salvation
- rank someone's holiness
- shame missed Crosses
- create compulsive streak mechanics
- compare users spiritually
- replace church
- replace Scripture
- replace prayer
- replace pastoral care
- present AI as spiritual authority

Fourtold is a tool.

It is not a mediator between God and the user.

### Identity
